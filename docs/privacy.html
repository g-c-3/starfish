Arkly privacy policy

Arkly is an Android app. This page says what it does with your data.

Your data stays on your phone

Notes, voice recordings, images, PDFs, files, money entries, locations, reminders and tags are stored only on your device. Arkly has no account, no server and no analytics, and the developer receives none of your data.

Permissions

  • Microphone and camera: only when you record or take a photo inside the app.
  • Location: only when you save your current location.
  • Notifications: to ring the reminders you set.
  • Biometrics: only to unlock the app with your fingerprint. The fingerprint stays in Android and is never read by the app.

Google Drive backup (optional)

Drive backup is off until you tap Connect Google Drive and choose a Google account. Arkly asks for one permission, drive.file, which lets it see and manage only the files and folder it creates itself. It cannot see the rest of your Drive.

Backups are encrypted on your phone with a passkey that only you know, before anything is uploaded. The passkey is never stored or sent anywhere. The backup goes to your own Google Drive and nowhere else. Tap Disconnect in Settings to stop; you can also remove Arkly's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other network use

The Check for updates button in Settings asks GitHub for the latest release when you tap it. Nothing is sent except the request itself. Arkly makes no other network connection.

Sharing and deleting

Arkly shares nothing on its own. Files leave the app only when you export, share or back them up. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your phone. Backups you made stay where you saved them until you delete them.
